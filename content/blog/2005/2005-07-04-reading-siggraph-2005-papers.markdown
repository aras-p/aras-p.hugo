I'm reading Siggraph 2005 preprint papers that are nicely gathered [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20050422020149/http://www.cs.brown.edu:80/~tor/sig2005.html). Some of them are really amazing! What I'm impressed with so far:

[Texture Montage](https://web.archive.org/web/20051019042641/http://www.research.microsoft.com/users/kunzhou/): looks cool. Just made me realize how primitive the "usual" texturing tools are... It makes me want to create some texturing tool...

[LDPRT](https://www.ppsloan.org/) and [Local PRT](https://web.archive.org/web/20050608010258/http://graphics.ucsd.edu:80/papers/plrt/) are steps in the needed direction for precomputed radiance transfer. Though [Ignacio](http://castano.ludicon.com/) says that CPCA compression of SH is patented my M$... Bah!

[Relational Debugging for the Graphics Pipeline](https://web.archive.org/web/20050206212658/http://www.cs.jhu.edu:80/~cohen/publications.html) is like NVPerfHud on steroids!

Reading on...
